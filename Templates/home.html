About SmartLMS

SmartLMS is a digital Library Management System designed to make reading and resource management effortless. It helps librarians, students, and faculty manage books, track borrowings, and explore a digital library experience.

Key Features:

  • Search and browse books easily
  • Borrow and return books online
  • Track due dates and avoid late fines
  • Admin panel for book and record management
  • Seamless digital reading experience

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register?

Click on Login and then choose “Sign Up” to create your SmartLMS account.

2. How can I borrow a book?

After logging in, search for the book and click Borrow.

3. What happens if I return a book late?

A small fine may apply for overdue returns as per library policy.

4. Can I suggest new books?

Yes! Use the contact form above to share your suggestions.

5. Who can I contact for support?

For technical issues, email us at support@smartlms.com.
